# Contact Us

We're here to help. Whether you have a question before you start, need a hand with a design, or have a problem with an order, get in touch and we'll get back to you.

### Email

For support and general enquiries, write to us at:

[support@weddingcrestdesign.com](mailto:support@weddingcrestdesign.com)

We usually reply within 24–48 hours.

### Before You Write

To help us reply faster, please include:

- the email address on your account;
- your order or project reference, if your question is about a purchase;
- a short description of what happened, plus a screenshot or the image you were working with if it helps.

### Common Topics

- **A design didn't match my description** — tell us the style, layout, and palette you selected so we can help you adjust the prompt.
- **I paid but didn't receive my files** — send us the email on your account and the approximate time of purchase.
- **My images still have a watermark** — watermarks are removed automatically once the Wedding Image Pack is unlocked for your project.
- **Deleting my account or my data** — see our [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy), or simply email us and we'll take care of it.
