## Introduction

Welcome to **Wedding Crest Design** ([weddingcrestdesign.com](https://weddingcrestdesign.com)), an AI-powered service for creating custom wedding crests, monograms, and wedding logos. Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and protect it, and the choices you have when you use our website and services.

## Information We Collect

1. **Account Information**
   - **What we collect**: your name, email address, and the profile details you provide when you sign up or sign in.
   - **Purpose**: to create and manage your account, deliver your designs, and provide customer support.

2. **Wedding Details and Photos You Provide**
   - **What we collect**: the names, initials, wedding date, style, layout, and colour palette you enter, plus any reference photos you choose to upload when generating a crest.
   - **Purpose**: these inputs are required to generate your custom crest design.

3. **Generated Designs and Project Data**
   - **What we collect**: the crest images and variations generated for you, together with the projects saved in your account.
   - **Purpose**: to let you revisit, download, and manage your designs.

4. **Usage Details**
   - **What we collect**: information about how you use the service, such as the pages you visit, the features you use, and how often you use them.
   - **Purpose**: to understand how the service is used and to improve it.

5. **Device and Log Information**
   - **What we collect**: device type, operating system, browser type, IP address, and similar technical data.
   - **Purpose**: to keep the service secure, compatible, and reliable.

6. **Cookies**
   - **What we collect**: small data files placed on your device that keep you signed in and remember your preferences.
   - **Purpose**: to operate core features and personalise your experience. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

7. **Payment and Billing Information**
   - **What we collect**: the billing details and payment identifiers needed to process a purchase.
   - **Purpose**: to charge for the Wedding Image Pack and to issue receipts. Card numbers are handled by our payment providers; we do not store full card details on our servers.

## How We Use Your Information

We use the information described above to:

- generate your crest designs and deliver the images you request;
- create and manage your account and your saved projects;
- process payments and prevent fraud;
- respond to your feedback and support requests;
- maintain, protect, and improve the service;
- comply with our legal obligations.

## AI Processing and Third-Party Model Providers

Wedding Crest Design uses third-party AI model providers to generate your crest images. Crests are generated with **Google's Gemini image generation models** (the "Nano Banana" family), accessed through the **Runware** inference platform; we may update or replace the underlying model over time. When you start a generation, your prompt, the wedding details you entered, and any reference photos you uploaded are transmitted to those providers solely to produce the images you requested. We do not authorise these providers to use your content for their own purposes beyond generating your design. AI-generated results vary between runs, so please review each design before you use it.

## Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only:

- with service providers that help us run the service — such as hosting, AI model, email, analytics, and payment providers — under contracts that limit their use of your data;
- to comply with legal obligations or valid requests from public authorities;
- to protect the rights, property, or safety of Wedding Crest Design, our users, or others.

## Data Retention

We keep your account information and saved designs for as long as your account is active. You can delete a design from your account at any time. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete or anonymise your personal information, except where we are required to retain it for legal, tax, or fraud-prevention purposes.

## Data Storage and Security

We take the security of your personal information seriously and apply industry-standard measures, including encryption in transit and access controls, to protect it. However, no method of transmission over the internet, and no method of electronic storage, is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## International Transfers

We and our service providers may process your information in countries other than the one you live in. Where required by law, we put appropriate safeguards in place for these transfers.

## Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete the personal information we hold about you, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To make a request, email us at [support@weddingcrestdesign.com](mailto:support@weddingcrestdesign.com). We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law.

## Children's Privacy

Wedding Crest Design is not intended for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children below the age at which they can consent to data processing in their jurisdiction. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.
