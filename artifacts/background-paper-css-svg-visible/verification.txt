Visible paper texture verification

Root cause found
- The previous pseudo-element was present but its effective contrast was too low to be visible in the rendered homepage.
- The page root was made a positioned layer above the fixed noise layer so the texture remains a background effect without intercepting input.

Implementation
- body background: two soft radial gradients plus #f6f6f4.
- body::before: fixed SVG feTurbulence noise, 200px tile, opacity .42, mix-blend-mode:multiply, pointer-events:none.
- body > *: position relative; z-index:1.
