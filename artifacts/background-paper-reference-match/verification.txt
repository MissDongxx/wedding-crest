Reference-matched paper texture verification

Reference analysis
- Empty reference regions sampled from the supplied image measured approximately RGB(245,245,243).
- Empty paper regions had local grayscale standard deviation approximately 2.8.
- The visual pattern contains a broad uneven paper field plus fine fiber grain.

Implementation
- Base color: #f5f5f3.
- Body: two subtle radial gradients for broad paper unevenness.
- body::before: two tiled SVG feTurbulence layers:
  - coarse cloud: 420px tile, baseFrequency .035, 3 octaves
  - fine fiber: 200px tile, baseFrequency .8, 4 octaves
- Noise layer: opacity .28, multiply blend, pointer-events none.
- body children remain above the background layer with z-index 1.
